# Welfare Fraud Detection

## Context

This case study shows how TRACE can be applied to a representative DPI-AI deployment in **social protection**.

## Core governance risks

- False positives can suspend livelihoods at scale.
- Caseworker over-trust can turn triage into de facto adjudication.
- Appeals often arrive after harm has already landed.

## Controls and evidence emphasis

The operational focus should be on explicit mandate boundaries, reversible workflows, transparent notices, and durable evidence capture. The associated operational artifacts should be sourced from the companion artifacts repository.

## Practical lessons

- Use strict separation between triage and final decision authority.
- Track reversals, complaint spikes, and locality clusters as first-class signals.
- Require evidence bundles before deployment and after major retraining.
