# Credit Scoring AI in Public-Interest Finance

## Context

This case study shows how TRACE can be applied to a representative DPI-AI deployment in **financial inclusion**.

## Core governance risks

- Proxy discrimination can hide inside feature engineering and external data purchases.
- Thin-file populations can be excluded by design while the model still looks accurate on average.
- Vendor claims are often richer than vendor evidence.

## Controls and evidence emphasis

The operational focus should be on explicit mandate boundaries, reversible workflows, transparent notices, and durable evidence capture. The associated operational artifacts should be sourced from the companion artifacts repository.

## Practical lessons

- Demand lineage and subgroup performance evidence.
- Treat model cards without remediation plans as marketing pamphlets in a nice shirt.
- Tie deployment approval to adverse-action notice quality and meaningful review rights.
