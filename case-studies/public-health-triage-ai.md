# Public Health Triage AI

## Context

This case study shows how TRACE can be applied to a representative DPI-AI deployment in **public health**.

## Core governance risks

- Resource allocation pressure can make weak models look irresistible.
- Data drift during outbreaks can invalidate prior calibration quickly.
- Operational urgency can erode auditability.

## Controls and evidence emphasis

The operational focus should be on explicit mandate boundaries, reversible workflows, transparent notices, and durable evidence capture. The associated operational artifacts should be sourced from the companion artifacts repository.

## Practical lessons

- Predefine escalation thresholds and model retirement triggers.
- Monitor false negatives and queue-priority distortions, not just global accuracy.
- Independent clinical and governance review should be built into live operations.
