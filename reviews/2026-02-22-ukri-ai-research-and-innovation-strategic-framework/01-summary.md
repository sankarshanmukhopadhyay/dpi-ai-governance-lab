# Summary

- **Citation:** UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) (2026-02-19), *UKRI AI Research and Innovation Strategic Framework*. https://www.ukri.org/publications/ukri-artificial-intelligence-research-and-innovation-strategic-framework/ukri-ai-research-and-innovation-strategic-framework/
- **Scope:** National R&I strategy for AI; reviewed as a system-level governance strategy with downstream implications for regulated-sector deployments (not a deployment spec).
- **Key claims:**
  - Defines six action areas spanning capability, adoption, infrastructure, skills, responsibility and security, and economic advantage.
  - Positions responsibility, security, and environmental impact as first-class requirements alongside growth and adoption.
  - Signals ambition for validated/assured AI systems in regulated sectors by 2031, but leaves operational binding mechanisms underspecified.
