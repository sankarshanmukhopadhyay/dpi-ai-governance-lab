# Methodology application

This review applies **TRACE (Trust, Risk, Architecture & Conformance Evaluation)** as defined in:

- Contract: `methodology/evaluation-contract.md`
- Workflow: `methodology/review-workflow.md`
- Rubric: `methodology/scoring-rubric.md`

## How TRACE was applied here

Because this document is a **national strategy** (not a system specification), the review:

- Treats “system” as the **policy + funding + delivery apparatus** implied by the framework (owners, incentives, accountability, and assurance pathways).
- Scores conservatively where the document signals intent without defining operational artifacts, controls, or evaluation methods.
- Records evidence using a lightweight claim extraction (`sources/notes.md`) and the prior draft review notes migrated from the legacy batch folder.

## Assumptions & boundaries

- This review does **not** validate empirical claims made by the framework.
- “Auditability” is evaluated as **whether the framework defines mechanisms** that would make delivery and assurance auditable (roles, artifacts, reporting, evidence), not whether audits have occurred.
