# Rebaseline next steps

The first real-review baseline gives a concrete evidence-derived backlog.

1. **Strengthen `CAP-AUTHORITY-BOUNDED-DELEGATION`.** It recurs in all three reviews and remains only partially covered by reusable artifacts.
2. **Instantiate standardized redress remediation.** Use a realistic deployment fixture and produce the required appeal, authority, SLA, and adverse-case evidence.
3. **Instantiate standardized evidence closure.** Produce a versioned evidence bundle with hashes, control mappings, and verification outcomes.
4. **Run executable negative vectors.** Test scope, revocation, stale/missing evidence, failed authorization correlation, and redress failure where applicable.
5. **Re-evaluate.** Record whether each gap remains open, moves to verification, closes, or leaves accepted residual risk.

New artifact development should be driven by repeated `coverage: partial` or `coverage: none` findings rather than catalogue expansion alone.
