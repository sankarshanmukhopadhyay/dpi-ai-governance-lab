# DPI AI Governance Maturity Model

This maturity model describes how an institution progresses from having policy language to having a living governance capability.

## Level 1 — Policy declaration
The organization can describe principles and intended safeguards, but artifacts are sparse and controls are mostly aspirational.

## Level 2 — Risk register
Material risks are identified, assigned owners, and reviewed periodically. The institution can name its worries in a structured way, which is already more civilized than pretending worries do not exist.

## Level 3 — Evidence bundle
Deployment and review decisions are backed by a defined bundle of evidence: controls, assessments, model documentation, incident pathways, and change records.

## Level 4 — Independent assurance
An internal or external reviewer can examine the deployment using portable artifacts, reproduce checks, and challenge claims.

## Level 5 — Continuous governance monitoring
The institution operates governance as an ongoing function with triggers, thresholds, remediation closure, and public accountability loops.

## How to use it

Use this model to set realistic release goals. Jumping directly from principle statements to high-trust deployment is how organizations end up inventing the governance equivalent of a cardboard submarine.
