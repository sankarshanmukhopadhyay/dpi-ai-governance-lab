# Digital Statecraft DPI corpus selection method

## Purpose

Freeze a bounded, reproducible first-wave corpus before TRACE evaluation so later findings cannot influence source selection.

## Inclusion criteria

Include posts that contain substantive propositions about one or more of:

- Digital Public Infrastructure architecture;
- public or institutional authority exercised through digital systems;
- registries, credentials, trust, reliance or data exchange;
- consequential digital decision infrastructure;
- redress, audit, accountability or correction;
- DPI + AI composition.

## Exclusion criteria

Exclude posts that are primarily general commentary and do not contain an operational DPI proposition that TRACE can evaluate.

## First-wave selection

The six selected posts intentionally span the proposition chain from DPI + AI, through authority and decision infrastructure, to inter-institutional reliance and remedy. The wave is small enough for consistent manual evidence review while broad enough to test cross-post recurrence.

## Evidence boundary

The corpus manifest records publication metadata and short selection rationales only. It does not introduce TRACE findings, scores, or claims about implementation gaps. Those belong to the evaluation stage.

No local copy of article text is committed in this stage. Canonical URLs remain the source of publication authority.
