# Scoring Scale (v1.0)

## Goal
Make scores consistent across papers and reviewers by anchoring each level to operational criteria.

## Scale
### 1 — Absent
- Dimension not addressed, or only implied.
- No actionable content.

### 2 — Mentioned / conceptual
- Dimension is named or discussed at a high level.
- No operational detail; no method to apply; evidence is weak.

### 3 — Partially operationalized
- Some operational components exist (partial method, partial mapping, partial metrics).
- Gaps prevent reliable implementation or audit without significant invention by the reader.

### 4 — Operational with gaps
- Mostly actionable; can be implemented or audited with reasonable effort.
- Gaps are identifiable and bounded (not systemic).

### 5 — Fully operational & auditable
- Clear, actionable, and evidence-backed.
- Includes artifacts/methods sufficient for implementation, evaluation, and audit.
- Explicit limitations acknowledged and handled via process or controls.

## Tie-break rule
If uncertain between two scores, score lower and record uncertainty in the audit trail.
